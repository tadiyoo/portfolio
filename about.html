About Me

Introduction

I am a computer science professional with a strong foundation in AI, machine learning, backend development, and data mining. I hold both a Bachelor’s (2021) and Master’s (2023) degree in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), where I consistently ranked first in academic achievements for six consecutive years. My research and hands-on experience include developing deep learning frameworks for multivariate time series anomaly detection, surgical workflow analysis, and triplet recognition in surgical video data. Additionally, I have worked on designing and implementing knowledge graphs for satellite data and AI applications. My technical skills, combined with my commitment to solving complex problems, drive my ambition to contribute meaningfully to innovative AI solutions across diverse industries.
